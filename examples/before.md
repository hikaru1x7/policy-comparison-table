# Remote Work Policy

## Section 1 Purpose

This policy defines the conditions under which employees may work remotely.
It applies to all full-time employees.

## Section 2 Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probationary period of six months are
eligible to request remote work.

## Section 3 Working Hours

Remote employees are expected to be available during core hours from
10:00 to 15:00.

## Section 4 Equipment

The company provides a laptop to each remote employee. Employees are
responsible for their own internet connection.

## Section 5 Termination

The company may revoke remote work arrangements at any time.
